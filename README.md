## user documentation + guide: Parent-Teacher Interview Night Reservation System
## general overview
This web application is designed for a teacher to easily organize parent-teacher night reservations, and for parents to easily reserve a slot.
The shared calendar lets both teacher and parents view available and taken slots on any day.
The parent slot list lets parents use the dropdown feature on the right to filter slots by teacher.

## guide for teacher
The teacher portal button on the home page leads user to a page where, at first, they will have a sign up option. The teacher puts their name, email, the courses they teach, and create a password. No verification codes are needed, you are automatically logged in.
Because this is designed for ONE teacher, once they create an account, the sign up feature is automatically removed, only leaving the log in page.
Once logged in, the teacher can view their "hub" or dashboard. Here, they can add slots for a specific date and time. 
If the slot(s) are booked, the teacher can see the parent and student's name listed on the booked slot.
Teacher can add and remove slots if needed.
Log out button for teacher is on top right.
On the home page, the teacher can also click "Teacher Portal" on the top right to log in.
Teacher can also view the calendar, see which days are full, and which days/slots are still open.

## guide for parents & guardians
On the home page, parents & guardians can view both the shared calendar and the slot overview. 
There are 2 ways to access the calendar: The purple "Open Calendar" button on the front home page, or the "Shared Calendar" on the top right.
There are also 2 ways to access and view the slots to book: The brown "View & Book Slots" button in the middle of the home page, or the "Parent Slot List" on the top right.
Once the reservation page is open, parents & guardians can use the dropdown option on the right to filter slots by course.
## booking / reservations for parents & guardians
When slots show up, it will be indicated whether it is an open or booked slot.
Booked slots will have a red "Taken" label, while open slots will have an "Available" label. These are the ones that are still open to be resesrved.
To book a slot, parents & guardians can click on the slot to reserve it. You will be asked to enter the Parent/Guardian's Name, and then the Student's Name.
Once the confirm button is pressed, you will be taken to a page confirming your slot reservation. From here, there will be a button underneath that will let you go back to the home page of the site.
## extra note on privacy
For privacy and security, only teachers can see the names added to the reservations. Parents who are booking will ONLY see the "Taken/Booked" label on the slot.
